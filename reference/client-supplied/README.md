# Client-supplied imagery

Kept for provenance. Neither set is served by the site — every image on the
site is rendered from the architect's drawings (see the root README).

## `hero-exterior.png` (1774 × 887)

The exterior visual the client supplied. It has the Walker Good Homes logo, a
headline and a gold CTA bar baked into it, so it cannot be used as a clean
photograph without cropping all three out of frame. The rendered exterior
views in `public/assets/cgi/` replace it.

## `preview-stills/` (540–930 px)

Ten interior stills recovered from the client's earlier sales clickthrough:
`kitchen`, `dining`, `living`, `hallway`, `cloakroom`, `bedroom-1`,
`bedroom-2`, `bedroom-3`, `bathroom`, `en-suite`. Preview resolution only, and
they do not follow the drawn room geometry — `bedroom-1` is the master,
`cloakroom` is the ground-floor W/C, and `hallway` and `en-suite` were doubling
for several rooms each.

They are useful as a reference for the intended styling of the fit-out. If the
originals are ever supplied at 2000 px or more, they could sit alongside the
renders as a styling reference rather than replacing them, since the renders
are the ones that match the drawings.
